House rules
===========

The scoring is the standard scoring. These are the bits every club settles for
itself, and this is how Gambit Hollow settles them.

Muggins
-------

We do not play muggins. If you miss points in your own hand they are gone, but
nobody else may claim them. The app should therefore always show the full count
for a hand at the show — there is nothing to be gained by hiding it and it saves
an argument.

The go
------

When neither player can lay a card without passing thirty-one, the last to lay
takes one for the go. Then the count resets to nought and the OTHER player
leads. Two for thirty-one exactly, and that also ends the count.

The show, in order
------------------

Non-dealer counts first, then the dealer, then the dealer's crib. That order
matters: a game can end mid-show and the non-dealer gets the chance first.

Winning
-------

A hundred and twenty-one, and the game stops the moment somebody reaches it —
mid-play, mid-show, wherever. There is no playing the hand out.

A tie
-----

Cannot happen. Only one player is ever counting at the moment the target is
reached, so whoever gets there first has won and the other stops.

The cut
-------

The cut card belongs to every hand at the show. It only helps a flush; it never
makes one on its own, and in the crib it must match all four for a flush at all.

His heels
---------

If the dealer turns a jack as the cut, the dealer takes two immediately, before
any card is laid. That is the cut scoring, not a hand scoring, so it is not part
of anybody's show.
