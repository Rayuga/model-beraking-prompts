Use the demo accounts from the hosting note. A login should create its own
unpredictable bearer session, stored by the server. Derive the current person
and their permissions from that session rather than from names or roles sent by
the browser. Signing out revokes that session, and protected data must stay
unavailable without a valid session.

The role rules in the interview policy apply on the server as well as in the
visible controls. Unknown people, vacancies, candidates and panel members must
be refused without changing the pipeline.
