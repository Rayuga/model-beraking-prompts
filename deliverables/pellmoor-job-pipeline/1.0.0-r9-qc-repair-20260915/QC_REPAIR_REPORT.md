## Zero-weight compatibility correction

The pinned `harbor-rewardkit==0.1.7` executes zero-weight judges, but its `_group_scores` originally reports a zero dimension score whenever the sum of that dimension's judge weights is zero. Merely changing the TOML weights would therefore have made a successful Oracle fail the final Render/Constraints gate.

`tests/rewardkit-compat.py` is applied during the verifier image build. For a dimension containing exactly one judge, it preserves that judge's actual aggregated criterion score regardless of the dimension's reward weight. The cross-dimension reward aggregation still uses the configured weights. Multi-judge aggregation is unchanged. The fix validates the pinned package version and exact expected source fragment, refuses an unknown implementation, and is idempotent.

The correction does not invent criterion results or force any dimension to pass. The actual RewardKit scheduling and aggregation were exercised with clearly labeled synthetic judge boundary inputs: all-pass gates remain 1; a failed gate remains 0; mixed gates remain partial; malformed scores are rejected by the unchanged final postprocessor. All five judges execute in every scheduling test.
