We're Ridgeline Press, a small risograph print studio, and we'd like a shop for our prints. People should be able to browse and order without an account. We don't take payment through this shop, so there's no card form to build.

The catalogue is in /assets/seed_data.json, with the photographs in /assets/prints. Please read our notes in /instructions before you start. They explain the paper sizes, trade prices and postage, including a few details that are easy to get wrong when someone changes their basket.

A grid would make browsing easy. Customers need to search by title, narrow things down by paper size or stock sheet, and sort by title or price. They should see the price and whether a print is available before opening it. The detail view can give the photograph more room and explain the sizes, paper, quantities and trade offer.

We sell small runs, so the stock matters. Two people might try to buy the last copy, or someone might press the order button again because their connection dropped. Please make sure we don't promise the same sheet twice or create two orders for that repeated attempt. The basket should stay with that visitor if they reload the page.

At checkout, customers need to see their delivery address and how the full-price subtotal, trade saving and postage make up the amount payable. Once they've ordered, give them a reference they can look up later. They can cancel before dispatch; their receipt should stay available and the sheets should go back into stock only once.

It should be comfortable to use on a phone or with a keyboard. We'd like light and dark modes too. Please deliver the finished shop in /app and follow /instructions/integration.md for the supplied setup.
