# Presentation review, round 2

The functional rules, gates, golden app and shared scoring tools remain unchanged from the previously validated candidate. This pass changes only the polish and visual judge descriptions and prompts.

Polish now has four equally weighted, objective usability checks: usable mobile catalogue/basket, meaningful labels and visible focus, ordinary search/add-to-basket feedback, and readable themes with usable detail/basket navigation. It does not require a complete keyboard-only purchase. No order is placed or cancelled for polish; its temporary basket is cleared afterward.

Visual keeps typography, colour/contrast, spacing/layout, hierarchy/scannability, overall consistency and responsive presentation. Its integer 1–5 anchors allow a clear and coherent conventional interface to earn full credit. Minor harmless imperfections, native components and simple styling are valid. No invented branding, artwork, animation or layout is required. A non-rendering surface receives raw 1, which the installed RewardKit 0.1.7 normalizes to zero.

The visual walk-through uses currently available products and the disclosed historical receipt. It does not depend on a receipt created in another isolated browser session or place/cancel an order merely to inspect appearance.

All 16 functional criteria and their internal weight of 35 remain intact. Transactional checkout, retry identities, cancellation, authoritative pricing, concurrency and restart remain difficult product checks. Scoring still uses functional 60%, polish 20%, visual 20%, with the same functional floor.

The earlier 203 backend assertions, 19 arithmetic cases, 9 primary browser groups, 5 resilience groups, 4 harness cases and 13 scorer fixtures apply to the unchanged source they tested. They are reused evidence, not claimed to have been rerun. The round-2 source audit executes 79 local assertions. All 53 workbook judgment rows and 48 deterministic inventory names are represented in `QC_REVIEW.xlsx`; final archive binding is recorded separately when packaging finishes.

Neither the private official checkers, platform QC, paid Oracle nor target-model runs were measured in this review. Presentation source quality and local golden evidence cannot guarantee a model judge's score.
