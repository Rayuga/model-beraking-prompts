- Every negative/refusal criterion has a relevant successful control. Each ordinary Functional/Polish/Visual failure stays local, except the explicit global prerequisite. Later stages tolerate gate-created and Functional-created records and do not demand a pristine count.

Once both gates pass and the strict Functional floor is cleared, perfect Polish/Visual contribute 0.4. Therefore a nonzero 0.1–0.4 score is not compatible with full presentation credit under this policy; model difficulty cannot be guaranteed by making the prose stricter.

## Limits and dispositions

The follow-up confidentiality redirect finding supersedes the initial no-source-change disposition; the Functional judge and prompt were corrected, preserving all counts and weights. The following remain explicit limitations, not measured successes:

1. Browser evidence cannot uniquely prove TypeScript/React/Vite/Express/SQLite, exact private implementation structure, or absence of all server-side evaluation. Those declared architecture/runtime requirements need source/build/harness evidence; successful browser requests alone are not proof. The prior coverage report already distinguishes them.
2. The confidentiality check samples three paths and at most 64 KiB per response, with conservative positive signatures. Unusual encodings, other paths or unrecognized content are outside that bounded observation. It closes the reported static-root counterexample without proving universal file secrecy.
3. All named unsupported execution families are covered, but this is not an exhaustive sandbox penetration test. The brief itself excludes arbitrary native blockers and generated machine code from its time-limit promise.
4. The Functional judge has many real actions in a 9000-second budget. Local browser feasibility and arithmetic do not measure a complete paid judge's duration, tool decisions or all five actual dimension verdicts.
5. Visual anchor 5 and natural-voice/platform rubric judgments remain judgments. The platform's private checker implementation and paid Oracle/model results were not executed by this reviewer. No claim of guaranteed acceptance or Oracle 1.0 follows from this report.
