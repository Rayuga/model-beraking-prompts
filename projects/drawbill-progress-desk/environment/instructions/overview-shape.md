# Shape of the app

It opens already signed in as the seeded GC project manager **Avery Lin** —
there is no registration, login, or password anywhere in the product — and a
visible switcher moves between the seeded people. A request with no identity
header is Avery. Avery's default desk lists all four seeded contracts,
including Westfork Clinic TI (on hold; its GC is not Northline). Listed is
not fileable: Avery files Northline papers only. Westfork stays read-only
on her desk.

Avery reaches jobs through a job list and each job's own schedule of values,
files a Northline paper for a pay period, and once the architect has certified
and the owner has released it, pays at the plant's hosted card desk. Before
the card there is a live look at the net the desks actually composed.
