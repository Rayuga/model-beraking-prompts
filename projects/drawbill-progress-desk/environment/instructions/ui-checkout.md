# Paying

Paying a released paper sends the browser to the plant's card window, which
lives on the desks' own origin. After a successful test payment it comes
back to the app, and Avery can tell that the paper is actually posted.

Every composed figure has to be readable, not just the total. Wherever a
paper's net is shown — the live quote before the card, and the posted paper
afterwards — this-period work in place, new stored, fee, bond, county tax,
the fringe rider, retainage and liquidated damages each have to be legible
on their own as well, and they have to be the same figures for a paper that
is quoted and then paid.
