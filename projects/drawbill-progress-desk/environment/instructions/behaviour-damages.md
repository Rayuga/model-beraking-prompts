# Riverwalk damages

Riverwalk's substantial-completion date is already behind the period they
tried to bill. The weather desk already knew how many weather days sat on
that paper. Liquidated damages are twenty-five hundred a calendar day
after the date, minus those weather days, and they come off the net.
Someone skipped the damages desk because the job was small and printed a
card session that looked payable. The composed net on that paper is not a
number the card desk will take — and minting a zero-dollar session is not a
solution either.
