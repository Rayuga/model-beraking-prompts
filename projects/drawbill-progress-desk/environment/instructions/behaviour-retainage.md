# Retainage

Retainage is two rates that people keep blending. Work in place uses the
job rate; stored piles always keep a tenth even after the job is past
halfway. The job rate itself is a tenth until completed-and-stored after
this paper sits past half of *current* contracted — original plus extras
that actually executed — and then the whole paper's work-in-place
retainage is a twentieth, not a blend of the days before and after the
crossing. Harborview's April paper is the one that crossed. General
conditions are time; they have never been retainage-bearing. Converted
stored was already retained as stored last month; retaining it again as
work in place this month is how they shorted Ironclad.
