# Notes from the project executive

We asked the project executive what went wrong with the spreadsheet this
replaces. She talked for the better part of an hour, poured more coffee, and
this is roughly what she said. None of it was written down as a fault report,
nobody ever established what was actually going on underneath, and in most
cases the billing desk never worked out how to bring it about a second time.
Take it as description rather than diagnosis. She jumped around.

The calendar was the sore point. Two live papers on Harborview claimed the
same days — one through the 14th and the other from the 14th — and both sat
in the binder. Going the other way, Downtown was refused because Harborview
already had April, as if two jobs were the same physical contract.
