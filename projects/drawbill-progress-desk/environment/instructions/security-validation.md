# Validation

Turn away what does not make sense before it reaches the database. A
date with a time or a timezone stuck to it, or written with slashes or
the zeros left off, is not a period day. A city typed onto a Harborview
quote does not get to pick Downtown's tax office, and a net, a
retainage rate, a fee, a fringe dollar, a previous-billed column, or a
payee list typed onto a request does not get to replace what the plant's
own records and the live desks say.
