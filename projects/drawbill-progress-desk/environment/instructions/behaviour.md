# How the plant works

This is the plant talking about their business. It is not a specification and
it does not describe an implementation. What the software has to guarantee is
for you to work out.

## Demo users

- Seed companies, people, jobs, and the plant from `/assets/artifacts/`.
  Artifact money is given in whole dollars. Copy those files into `/app`.
- A fresh browser is already Avery Lin. A switcher changes who is using the
  app. There are no passwords.
- The switcher is there so the GC can demo the product to people.

## Billing

Northline files its own owner papers and pays at the card desk after the
architect has certified and the owner has released the paper to the rail.
Nobody else collects the owner card.
