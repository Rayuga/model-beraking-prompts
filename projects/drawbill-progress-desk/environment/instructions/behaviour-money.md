# What the owner actually pays

Finance stopped treating the SOV grid sum as the card total, and they do
not treat a file on the tablet as the books — they read SQLite. The seed
cards are still written in whole dollars; the books themselves keep the
pennies, and more than one forty-thousand-dollar mill extra landed in the
ledger as four hundred dollars because someone copied the card number across
as-is.

A paper is not a typed "this period." It is this month minus last posted
month. Someone typed this-period percentages onto a request that already had
a previous billed column, and the till believed the request. Last month's
posted snapshot did not move. Quotes that never paid do not count as last
month either — Avery watched an abandoned Harborview May session leave a
ghost row that then subtracted from April.
