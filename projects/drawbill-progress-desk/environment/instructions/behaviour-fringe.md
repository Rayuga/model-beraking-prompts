# Fringe rider

Helen's fringe rider sits on the card total. The county has never seen it,
retainage has never seen it, and fee has never seen it. April's Harborview
rider is whatever the fringe desk already has for that job and those
inclusive days — do not type hours at them. March's rider already posted;
do not invent a second March. A paper that forgot the rider paid a
different card total than the one that remembered it, and a paper that
taxed it paid a third.
