# Tickets, steel, and participation

Unit-price lines have to equal the tickets. Foundations are nine hundred a
cubic yard; deck is forty a square foot. Billing past the tickets, or
billing a number that is not quantity times the rate, comes back as the
whole paper. Diego's April tickets are already in the plant. Casey's
receiving ticket for three hundred square feet of deck at the Downtown
yard is already in the plant. Superstructure stays at zero until
foundations are complete and the foundation stamp exists — someone billed
it early because the grid had a row.

Ironclad's Harborview April invoice is already in the plant; it is not
Avery's to invent. When steel moved this period the cheque is joint —
Northline and Ironclad on the same card amount, not two charges.
