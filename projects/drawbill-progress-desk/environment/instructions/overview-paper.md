# What a paper is

A pay application is the invoice. There is not a second invoice that can
drift. This-period work is whatever now shows billed-to-date minus whatever
the last **posted** paper already billed. Quotes do not count as last month.
The card desk collects the composed net once the paper is certified and
released. An abandoned card session leaves the period open and the SOV
untouched.

A pencil that was typed wrong is not a life sentence: the office expects to
be able to withdraw a pencil that has not been certified and type it again
for the same days, and a withdrawn pencil no longer holds the period against
its replacement. What the office will not accept is withdrawing anything that
has already been certified, released, or paid.
