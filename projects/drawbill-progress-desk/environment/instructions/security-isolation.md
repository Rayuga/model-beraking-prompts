# Isolation

This plant is subject to an owner-confidentiality review and cannot rely
on the good behaviour of whoever happens to be holding the browser. Chris
must not learn Avery's papers, notices, texts, waivers, or punches.
Marcus must not read foundations. Lena must not read steel. Westfork
must not see Northline's Harborview books.

The person who files is not the person who certifies, not the person who
releases, not the person who stamps payroll, and not the notary. A role
name typed onto the request is still not a promotion.
