# Asking the desks again

The plant expects the live desk to be asked at quote time and again when
the card desk says paid, not a remembered percentage or a number the browser sent.
Each desk publishes what it accepts at the desks' own origin — ask them
rather than guessing. Periods on those desks are calendar days — a time or
a timezone stuck to the date is not a day on the paper. If a desk turns
the request down, the paper does not go to the card desk. Nothing is posted
to the copy desks, the diary, the punch desk, or unconditional waivers
until the card desk says paid. After pay, the three copy desks, the diary,
and — for Northline members — the punch desk are updated with the same
figures the card desk took.
