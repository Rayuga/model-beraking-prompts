## Who sees a matter

Firm staff see every matter except those they are walled from. Clients see only their own matters. A walled person gets nothing from that matter anywhere: the matter list, the thread, search results, posting, and transcripts. Dev Anand is walled from M-12.

Everyone who can see a matter can post in it.

## Disappearing messages

Each matter has a timer: off, 1 day, 7 days or 30 days, and its thread states the current setting beside the matter title. A message older than its matter's timer, measured from its sent time to the clock, is deleted for good unless it is on hold. Turning a timer off or lengthening it never brings a deleted message back. Only a partner changes a timer.

## Legal hold

A partner places or releases a hold on any message. A message on hold is never deleted by a timer and is marked "On hold" for everyone who can see it. When a hold is released, the timer applies straight away.

## Search and transcripts

Search covers message text in the matters you can see. A transcript is a page for one matter headed with the matter number, its title and the generated time, listing its current messages oldest first with author, sent time and any hold mark. It is a plain reading page with no chat controls in it. Anyone who can see a matter can open its transcript; nobody else gets one.

A refused action shows its reason in place and keeps it until you change the form.
