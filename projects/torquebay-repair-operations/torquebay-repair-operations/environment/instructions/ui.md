# What it should feel like

Routes, labels and layout are yours. A person has to find their way around
by reading the screen. Give each role a landing view that matches their
day: advisors need tickets, quotes and the bay board; technicians need
assigned jobs; the manager needs approvals, money and history; parts and
warranty need their queues; customers need a focused portal.

Status, whose job it is, money, bay state and anything waiting on a
decision should be obvious without hunting.

It should feel considered on a desktop and still usable on a phone. Use
headings and labels a person can follow, and say clearly when something
worked or was refused.
