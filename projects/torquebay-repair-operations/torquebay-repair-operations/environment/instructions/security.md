# Who can do what

There is no login screen. The switcher picks which seeded person you are,
and if nobody is picked you are Nora.

A customer should only see their own account. They should not be able to
open someone else's vehicle, ticket, estimate, claim, invoice, or
appointment.

Advisors take intake and write estimates up to their usual limit. The shop
manager handles the big approvals and the history. The floor dispatches
work only to a technician certified for that job. Parts may reserve stock
only when it fits the vehicle on the ticket. Warranty handles claims, but
the person who filed a claim cannot also approve or deny it — someone else
on warranty has to take that look.

When something actually changes, there should be a history row saying who
did it, to what, and when.
