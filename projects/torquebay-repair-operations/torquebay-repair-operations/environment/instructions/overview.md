# Who is on the desk

TorqueBay is the working desk for a busy multi-bay shop. The seed files are
how the morning starts: customers and vehicles, a mixed staff roster,
occupied and tagged-out bays, parts on the shelf, and repair orders already
in motion.

Nora Adler is the service advisor people land on. The switcher should also
make the day readable for the shop manager, a technician, parts and warranty
staff, and each customer. Staff see the work that belongs to their role.
Customers see only their own vehicles, estimates, balance, and appointment
requests.

The usual day is intake, a quote, the customer saying yes, a second staff
sign-off, a bay and a certified technician, parts that fit, or warranty if
needed, then finish and bill. Existing tickets should still make sense
after those records change. Money in the JSON is whole US dollars, and the
approval limits in the seed are the ones people actually have.
