# How the floor actually goes

These are the things people keep bumping into, not a build recipe.

Nora opens tickets and writes estimates. A customer can approve their own
pending quote. The person who wrote an estimate cannot also give it the
shop sign-off. Anything expensive waits on the shop manager.

Somebody once booked a second job over a bay that was already busy, and
another person parked work in a bay that was tagged out. A technician
got assigned to a job they were not certified for. Parts reserved a
part that does not fit the vehicle on the ticket. A warranty claim got
reviewed by the same person who filed it.

Finished work sometimes never made it to an invoice, and once an
unfinished ticket got billed anyway. A customer asked for an appointment
on a vehicle that was not theirs.
