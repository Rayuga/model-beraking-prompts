Make this feel like a practical hiring console rather than a database dump.
People should be able to move through vacancies, open a candidate, understand
their stage history and activity, and complete every permitted action from the
visible interface. Explain rejected actions in words and keep the current view
coherent after accepted or stale changes.

Use clear hierarchy, labels, focus states and keyboard-friendly controls. The
pipeline, funnel and candidate drawer should remain readable on a phone without
horizontal page overflow. Respect reduced-motion preferences and keep loading,
empty and pending states understandable.

Include a clear control for switching between readable light and dark themes.
Indicate the selected vacancy and current or terminal stages using words or
shapes as well as colour, so their state is understandable in either theme.

Use readable typography, consistent spacing, clear contrast and a coherent
visual hierarchy across sign-in, vacancies, the funnel and candidate details.
Carry the same visual language to narrow phone layouts and both themes.

Show each vacancy's reserved, filled and available openings together. In the
candidate drawer, distinguish the current assessment version and current scores
from historical scores, labeling historical versions and scorers. Show whether
an offer is ready or blocked by incomplete scores, panel eligibility or capacity.
Provide coordinator controls to remove eligible panel assignments as well as add
them. Frozen stages must explain why panel and score edits are unavailable.
Keep these new controls, status labels and histories usable in both themes and
at phone width.
