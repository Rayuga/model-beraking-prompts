# Zoning, finance, field work, and certificates

The district zoning officer approves only a plans-approved permit whose stored
parcel permits its stored type. That action creates one assessment from the
stored permit fee plus statutory levy.

A clerk may request a non-negative waiver no greater than the permit fee. A
waiver never reduces the levy. The requester cannot approve it. A waiver of
$500 or less needs a district supervisor; a waiver above $500 needs both the
district supervisor and Finance. Finance records one exact outstanding receipt
with a retry-safe key; overpayment, underpayment, and duplicate settlement are
refused while a retry with the same key yields the original record.

After zoning approval and settlement, the district supervisor assigns an
in-district inspector. Only the assigned inspector schedules and records field
results. Foundation must precede Final, and one inspector cannot hold two
active inspections in the same time slot. A failed result returns the permit
to corrections and clears zoning and pass completion, while retaining the
failed field record as evidence.

The district supervisor issues one certificate only after zoning approval,
exact settlement, and passed Foundation and Final inspections. Certified and
denied records are terminal and offer no workflow-changing action.
