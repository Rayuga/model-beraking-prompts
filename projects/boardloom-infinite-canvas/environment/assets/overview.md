# What Boardloom is for

It is the whiteboard our design reviews happen on. Diagrams, wireframes, sticky
notes, the messy thinking that happens before anything gets built.

The thing we keep tripping over in the tool we use now is that it is everybody's
workspace at once. I want mine to be mine. You make an account with your own
password, you sign in, and what you see is the boards you made. Somebody else
signing in sees theirs, and there is no way to reach into mine — not by guessing
at a board, not by listing, not at all. Nobody who is not signed in sees
anything.

Passwords are at least eight characters. Signing up with an address that already
has an account should be turned away rather than quietly making a second one.
There is a Logout in the header that takes you back to the sign-in screen, and it
should actually be there — I have used tools where the only way out was clearing
cookies.

When you sign in for the first time, or straight after registering, put me on a
new empty Untitled board. Empty means empty: no demo shapes, no sample project,
no "Welcome to Boardloom!" sticky note. An empty canvas on first sign-in is
correct, not a bug.

Boards get created, renamed, archived and restored. Archiving is how we retire an
old review without losing it — an archived board can be read but not changed, and
it comes back when you restore it.

Two of us will be using it while you build:

- kai.editor@board.demo — KaiBoard#2026
- asha.editor@desk.demo — AshaDesk#2026

Neither exists yet. They get registered like anyone else.
