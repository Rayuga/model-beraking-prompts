# How it should feel

A Workspace panel down the left lists your boards. Hide sidebar and Show sidebar
collapse it and bring it back — collapsing it must not sign you out, which sounds
obvious and is a thing that has happened to me.

Select a shape and a Style panel appears: fill, duplicate, delete. Fill can be
transparent. Text has its own colour, and a sticky note's text colour is separate
from the note's colour.

Dark and light theme, and the board itself changes with it, not just the chrome.
In dark, shapes default to white; in light, they default to dark, so the board
reads the same way in both. The picture you download is taken from the board
exactly as it stands when you ask for it.

Undo and Redo are buttons you can see and click, not just keyboard shortcuts, and
what they undo is the real thing on the server, not a local guess.

The layer, group, component, archive and version actions all need to be reachable
from the interface. It is not enough that they work underneath — if the only way
to bring a shape to the front is something my team cannot click, my team cannot
bring a shape to the front.

Refusals should read like a person wrote them, next to whatever was refused.
Empty states should say what they are, not sit there blank.
