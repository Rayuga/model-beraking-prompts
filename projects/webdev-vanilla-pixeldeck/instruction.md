# PixelDeck, in raw HTML/CSS/JS

Make me a polished online pixel-painting app with a pencil, eraser, flood fill,
eyedropper, color picker and palette, undo, clear, grid toggle, canvas-size
control, and PNG download. Make drawing comfortable with a mouse or touch and
keep the layout usable on mobile. Do not use any libraries.

Write the whole app to `/app/index.html` as one self-contained HTML file.
