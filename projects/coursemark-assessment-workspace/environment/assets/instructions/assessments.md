# Assessment authoring and availability

Students see only published assessments in their enrolled course and never
receive stored answer keys. Staff may see drafts and item details. Only the
course instructor can create a draft, add questions, or publish it.

A new draft needs a title, opening time before its due time, a positive whole
minute duration, and a positive whole-number attempt limit. Drafts accept
multiple-choice and written questions with positive points. Multiple-choice
questions need at least two distinct options and a key matching one option;
written questions need a positive rubric criterion.

Publishing requires at least one question and a positive duration. It is
terminal: a published assessment cannot accept another question or be
published twice. Every accepted publish writes exactly one audit event; every
rejected create, item, or publish request leaves state and audit unchanged.
