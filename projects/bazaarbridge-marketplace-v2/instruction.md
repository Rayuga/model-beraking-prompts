# Task: BazaarBridge — marketplace operations

Build BazaarBridge, a full-stack multi-vendor marketplace operations app for
a team that runs three merchants' catalogs, orders, warehouses, finance,
returns and compliance work. It must be a real server with durable storage,
not a static dashboard. Nine people use it and nobody is allowed to do
everything.

## Where the brief is

Everything the team has written down is under `/instructions/`: overview.md,
behaviour.md, security.md, integration.md, ui.md and policy.md. Each of those
files is one desk's own notes, so read all of them and treat the field notes as
complaints, not as a specification. The starting records are in
`/assets/artifacts/bazaarbridge_seed_data.json`.

Routes, labels and layout are yours. A person must be able to find their way
around by reading the UI. Satisfy yourself that it holds up in a browser: sign
in as each kind of person, change something real, then come back fresh and
check it is still the way you left it.
