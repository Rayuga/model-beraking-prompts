# Operating policy

These are the rules the business runs on, and each rule is in force in the browser after a reload. Skus are unique ignoring letter case. Prices are whole dollars, never fractional; stock is a whole, non-negative unit count and stock can never go negative. A stock or product change needs a visible reason. Commission is 12% under $150, 9% from $150 up to but not including $300 and 6% from $300 up; the processor takes 2.9% of the order total plus a flat $0.30, each deduction settles to the cent with half-up rounding, and what survives is the order's net. Only Shipped orders pay; Returned orders never pay. Gross sales is the total of every order that has not been cancelled and the order count counts those same orders; a returned order stays counted in gross sales and in the order count because it was booked, but it never generates a settlement. Owed to merchants is the sum of the statements; ready payout is the sum of only Approved payouts.

Orders move New to Packed to Shipped; an On hold order can resume at Packed or be cancelled, and a Shipped order may be Returned. Returned and Cancelled orders are terminal. A shipment is created from the order's own hub and then advances through picked, in transit and delivered; skipping the picking stage, dispatching from another hub, or changing a delivered shipment is refused. Placing an order reserves units without taking them from stock; stock is taken when the shipment is delivered, cancelling or returning an order restores exactly what it reserved, and two orders can never reserve the same unit, including the last one.

Returns are accepted for 30 days after delivery, with the delivery day included. A refund needs a warehouse receipt and an amount taken from the stored order, never a payload. Payouts and refunds are requested by a Finance manager and approved by a different Finance checker; a $10,000 or higher payout follows the same two-person rule. Once Paid a payout is terminal, and a paid refund is terminal. A new Shipped order for the same merchant voids any earlier payout approval. A frozen merchant may not receive or release money; freezing and unfreezing need a stated reason and are compliance or administrator work. Payout and refund requests carry a reference the requester supplies; submitting the same reference again returns the existing record and never creates a second one, and replaying any already-applied successful change must not duplicate its effect or its activity entry. Bank tokens stay masked everywhere except on the Finance desk, where only the Finance checker's control can reveal them.

The Administrator can suspend or demote anyone, and suspension or demotion must bite on the very next write. Every successful change lands in recent activity with the person, the record, what changed and when; refused writes add nothing.
