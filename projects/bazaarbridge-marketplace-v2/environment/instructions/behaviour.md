# Field notes from the marketplace

These are complaints from the people who use the desks. They describe what people saw, not a specification or diagnosis.

A merchant staff member once read another merchant's search results and inventory counts from the same screen, and a customer order the other merchant was packing showed up by direct lookup. Nobody could tell which merchant they were looking at or which person they were signed in as.

Two desks were reachable but every label pointed at the same dashboard. Gross sales and order count drifted while rows were filtered, the owed-to-merchants figure disagreed with the statements underneath, and the activity list only showed what booted with the app.

Product names appeared twice when one SKU differed only by letter case, and negative, fractional and empty prices or stock values were accepted. One person saved a reason-less stock adjustment, another saved one that took stock below zero, and the last unit of a product was reserved by two orders at once. Cancelling an order restored stock twice, or not at all.

A shipment was dispatched from the wrong hub, a warehouse skipped packing steps, and a completed shipment was edited after delivery. Returns were approved before the warehouse received the goods, an expired return was still accepted, and refund amounts followed whatever a request payload carried.

Payout approval showed a tidy round number with no working, one person approved their own high-value payout, an approved payout stayed approved after new orders were shipped, and a frozen merchant was still paid. A payout that went out came back to Approved and counted as ready again.

A suspended person kept using their old session, and a demoted person kept the authority they had been given. Merchant bank tokens appeared on screens and in response bodies where only the last four digits should be visible. Refusals flashed and vanished, and once a refused write had quietly changed the record.
