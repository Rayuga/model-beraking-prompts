Can you build HireOps, the workspace our recruiting team lives in all day? Two things happen in it. Candidates move across a pipeline board for each job, and recruiters talk to those candidates in a conversation attached to each application. Several of us have it open at once, so the hard part is that nobody's work gets trampled when someone else acts.

On the board, a card moves one stage at a time, and every move has to work with ordinary controls; dragging is welcome but optional. The order of cards inside a stage is ours to arrange and everyone sees the same order. Interview has a limited number of places per job. If a move is refused, put the card back where it really is and tell me why; when it is because a colleague moved that card a moment ago, the refusal should say who. I often move several candidates together; that has to be all of them or none, and I need to be able to undo my last move, single or several, as long as nobody has touched those cards since.

Other people's moves should show up on my board within fifteen seconds without a reload, and without closing the card I have open, wiping the note I am typing or moving my cursor.

Conversations are between our side and the candidate. A long one opens on the most recent messages and lets me pull in earlier ones without losing my place. If a message arrives while I am reading further up, leave my place alone and just tell me there is something new; whether I am reading or halfway through a reply, never touch my draft or my cursor. Unread counts need to be right for each person, and I want to see when the candidate has seen my last message. A reply I have not sent stays with that conversation when I look at another one, reload, or get signed out; when I sign back in it is still there, and nobody else who signs in on that browser sees it.

Candidates sign in too. They see only their own applications, described in the wording we use for candidates, and their own conversations. They must never see our internal notes, why someone was rejected, or anyone else.

The exact stage rules, who may do what, and the conversation rules are in /instructions/hireops_rules.md. Read it alongside /instructions/integration.md. We need Board, Conversations and Activity views for staff and a simple applications view for candidates; layout and names are yours. A clear desktop workspace is what matters. We don't need theme switching or a special phone layout.

Before you hand it over, please check it yourself the way we'll use it: sign in as two of us in separate browsers, move and reject cards, move a few together and undo, send messages back and forth, reload, and make sure nothing breaks.

Put the application in /app, built with Node.js, Express and SQLite, using the seed data at /assets/seed_data.json. It must start with node /app/server.js, listen on port 3000 (honour PORT if it's set), expose GET /api/health as a check that never touches the database, serve the UI as well as the API from that one process, and keep its durable data in /app/app.db, honouring DB_PATH when that is set. How you lay the other files and routes out under /app is your call. Server dependencies are already available, so don't install packages. The server itself mustn't depend on any outside service for its data. The browser side is free to pull fonts or a script from a CDN if that helps.

Copy what you need into /app; /assets and /instructions are unavailable at runtime. Load the supplied people, jobs, applications, messages, notes and read markers once, start with no shipped database, and keep new work across restarts.
