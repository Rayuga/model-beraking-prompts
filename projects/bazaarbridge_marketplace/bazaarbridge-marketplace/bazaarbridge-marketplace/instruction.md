# Task: BazaarBridge — marketplace operations

Build a full-stack web app called **BazaarBridge**, a multi-vendor marketplace
operations tool our team can use every day, with a real backend and durable
storage rather than a static dashboard. Three people share it and they are not
all allowed to do the same things.

## Where the brief is

Everything we have written down is under `/instructions/` — `overview.md`,
`behaviour.md`, `security.md`, `integration.md`, `ui.md` and `policy.md`.
Different people wrote different parts of it and each of them expects their part
to be honoured. `behaviour.md` is the operations team talking about their own
work, so read it as complaints rather than as a specification.

The records we start from are in
`/assets/artifacts/bazaarbridge_seed_data.json`.

Routes, labels, and file layout are yours to choose. A person must be able to
find their way around by reading the UI.

We have been handed demos before that looked finished and fell apart the first
time somebody actually used them, so satisfy yourself that this one holds up in
a browser rather than only in the code. Sign in as each of the people who use
it, work through the marketplace the way they would, change something real,
then come back to it fresh and check it is still the way you left it.
