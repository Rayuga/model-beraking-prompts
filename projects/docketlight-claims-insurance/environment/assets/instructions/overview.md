# What this is

We're a regional insurer. Every claim we take in has to be worked by a real person whose job decides what happens next, so I need one browser workspace where intake, adjusters, supervisors and finance sign in, see the work they are supposed to see, open a file, and take the next legitimate step. Today too much of that lives in inboxes and spreadsheets, and I want a single place the desk can trust.

Please use the sample data that came with this brief as our book of record for accounts, policies, claims, payments, ledger entries, and history. Those are our real-looking examples, so keep their identities and figures intact instead of substituting made-up ones.

The desk also has to be honest about where its state lives. What you read on screen and every change you make has to come from and go back to the server and its database: a page that only pretends to remember things, or that shows a figure the server never worked out, is worse than useless to me.
