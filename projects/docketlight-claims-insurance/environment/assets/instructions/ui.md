# What the desk should feel like

Please make something our people can actually use: a clear sign-in, a responsive workspace that shows who I am and which region I work, a summary derived from the records I can see, rows I can open, useful detail, and clear feedback when something works or is refused.

A claim row should show its reference, region, current state, and the payable exposure the server calculated. The reference on a newly created claim is the one the desk records for it, so that resulting reference must be readable on the claim row and in its opened detail.

Opening a row should show the customer, policy number, coverage dates and deductible, loss date and amount, payable exposure, assignee, evidence, reserve, payment, and ledger information where it applies, plus a chronological history of who did what and when. It's fine for this to live on one screen if rows open their details; you don't have to build extra pages. Payment and ledger lines shown with a claim belong to that claim, while the organisation-wide financial view belongs to Finance and Administrators.

Show the actions a role can validly take and keep settled claims readable without presenting a write path as successful. Please keep dense sections scannable on a desktop and usable at roughly phone width without sideways page overflow, with sensible headings, labels, keyboard focus, and accessible names. A refused action should say so plainly rather than looking like it worked.
