# BazaarBridge

We run BazaarBridge together: three merchant catalogs, eight desks, nine
people, and orders, stock, shipments, returns and money moving between them
all day. The application we have now keeps letting the wrong person see or
change the wrong thing, and we only notice after the fact.

Can you build the replacement for us? It has to be one real application with
durable storage that everyone signs into from their own laptop. The starting
records live in /assets/artifacts/bazaarbridge_seed_data.json, and our notes
are under /instructions/: overview.md, behaviour.md, security.md,
integration.md, ui.md and policy.md. Those files are what we wrote while
working, not a specification. Where a note complains about something
happening, we expect the app to stop it happening.

We have not picked routes, labels or layouts, and we do not intend to. We just
need an app someone can find their way around by reading it, and that still
shows what we changed when we sign in again later.
