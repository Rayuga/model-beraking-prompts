# What keeps happening

A merchant staff member once read another merchant's search results, inventory counts and an order the other merchant was packing, and nobody could tell which merchant they were looking at or who was signed in.

Two desks opened but every link showed the same dashboard. Headline sales and order counts drifted while rows were filtered, the owed figure disagreed with the statements underneath, and recent activity showed only what was present at boot.

Product names appeared twice when SKUs differed only by letter case. Negative, fractional and empty prices or stock values were accepted; a stock adjustment went through without a reason, another took stock below zero, the last unit was reserved by two orders at once, and cancelling an order restored stock twice or not at all.

A shipment went out from the wrong hub, one skipped the packing stage, and a completed shipment changed after delivery. Returns were approved before the warehouse received the goods, an expired return was still accepted, and refund amounts followed whatever the request payload carried.

Payout approval showed a round number with no working, someone approved their own high-value payout, an approved payout stayed approved after new orders shipped, a frozen merchant was still paid, and a paid payout came back to Approved and counted as ready.

A suspended person kept using their old session; a demoted person kept powers they no longer had; full bank tokens appeared where only the last four digits belong; refusals flashed and vanished; and a refused write had quietly changed a record.
