Its `Internal Quality Checks!D30` retains a historical cross-origin phrase, but `Quality Checks!D30`, the frozen skill's delivery profile, `qc/REVIEW_POLICY.md`, and the current assignment explicitly say public network and forbid a same-origin gate.

It requires the judge to author a fresh marker and positive integer addition, enter code, click Run, and observe the computed output in both preview and console. The prompt (`task/tests/gates/constraints/prompt.md:15-33`) explains clean-context setup and forbids same-origin restrictions.

Off-origin browser assets are explicitly allowed (`task/environment/instructions/integration.md`; `task/environment/instructions/policy.md`).
